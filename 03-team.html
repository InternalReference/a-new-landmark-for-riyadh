Our process

Stage 1Client brief and site analysis, to set the goals and the constraints.
Stage 2Moodboards and conceptual directions, then a concept layout, visualisations and renders.
Stage 3The chosen concept adapted to the space and layout, developed as plans and elevations.
Stage 4Detailed plans with specifications and materials, plus lighting, audiovisual and bespoke-element drawings.
Stage 5Bidding support, specialist suppliers, shop-drawing review, and coordination with the Architect of Record.
Stage 6Design oversight during assembly and construction on site.

02 · The Team

The consultant team.

A landmark supertall requires 30 to 40 specialist firms, across engineering, tall-building, environmental, commercial and statutory disciplines. The client works through two reporting lines: programme and cost on one side, design on the other. The lead design consultant heads the design line and coordinates every discipline within it.
