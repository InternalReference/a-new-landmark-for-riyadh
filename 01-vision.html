01 · Design Intention

Five requirements.

01

Iconic

A form that reads as a landmark in its own right, recognisable on the skyline.

02

Green

Physical, inhabited greenery. Planted private and shared outdoor space, engineered to survive Riyadh's climate.

03

Diverse

A deliberate mix of uses, each justified by demand, sized for resilience across market cycles.

04

Connected

An active base at street level that connects to and is used by the city, open to people who neither live nor work in the building.

05

Holistic

Clean air, movement, nourishment, care and nature, designed into every space and run as commercial uses.

02 · Site Selection

Main considerations.

Condition 01

Arterial frontage

In Riyadh, building height is only reviewed on roads of 40 metres or wider. Without that frontage, a supertall cannot be approved.

Condition 02

Acquirable land

Some of the strongest sites sit inside foundations or regulated funds and are not sold outright. The route to control the land matters as much as the location.

Condition 03

Minimum plot

About 3 hectares, no side shorter than about 80 metres, to carry the tower, its core, a generous podium and servicing.

Beyond thesethe strongest ground is where prestige, transit and the city's new green infrastructure meet.

Preferred

King Salman Park edge. The largest urban park in the region, on the metro, in the northern growth sector. Access is through partnership with the park's development structure, not an open purchase.

Fallback

A privately held parcel on a northern arterial, where acquisition is direct. Less green adjacency, full control.

SourcesRiyadh Building Regulations, Omrania.King Salman Park Foundation.

06 · Green

Green.

Physical, planted outdoor space at every home, targeting 200 percent greenery replacement.

  • 01
    PrecedentsBosco Verticale, WOHA's Singapore towers, Heatherwick's EDEN. All of them in mild climates.
  • 02
    AdaptedThe same, delivered in Riyadh's heat and water scarcity. Shade-first massing, native planting, irrigation on recycled water.
  • 03
    MaintenanceA thirty-year horticultural model, funded from the start. Greenery at height depends on maintenance over decades, which is where it usually fails.
  • 04
    DistinctionOne Gulf vertical forest announced in Dubai in 2022 remains unbuilt. Ours is defined by delivery, at supertall scale.

07 · Connected

The base as public ground.

The podium is where the tower meets the city. It should be used by people who neither live nor work in the building.

  • 01
    ClimateAn open public space in Riyadh needs shade and cooling to be used. The base is shaded, planted and evaporatively cooled, a ground people walk through.
  • 02
    A reason to comeCulture and food draw the public, the way the museum at Roppongi Hills or the promenade at Marina Bay Sands draw theirs.
  • 03
    The park's front doorOn a park-edge site the green does not begin in the sky. It begins at street level, as public ground that carries the park into the site.

08 · Holistic

Health is the new luxury.

Not gold and marble. The building is designed to generate health, and most of the systems that do also earn. Five of them, each a design decision and a commercial use.

01

Breathe

Low-emission materials and continuously filtered, monitored air. VOCs, formaldehyde and combustion removed at source.

Design valueSupports the residence premium

02

Move

A performance club, studios and courts, and a shaded outdoor route. Exercise is the most evidence-based measure of healthspan.

RevenueFitness membership

03

Nourish

Organic retail and health-led restaurants, supplied in part by an on-site farm.

RevenueRetail and food leases

04

Heal

A longevity centre for diagnostics, prevention, recovery and sleep. Not a hospital.

RevenueClinic membership and corporate health

05

Grow

The vertical forest, daylight and quiet. Greenery is a measured health input, not decoration.

Design valueThe green premium

The disciplineCertified to the WELL standard, not asserted. Unproven treatments are declined. The largest funder of healthspan research is based in Riyadh. Green is health.

SourcesGlobal Wellness Institute, wellness real estate.WELL Building Standard, IWBI.Hevolution Foundation, Riyadh.

The approach

Proven. Better. New.

Proven

Every element has been built before. Supertall structure, mixed use, vertical forests, an activating podium, and the demand behind each. Nothing here is speculative.

Better

Each is taken to a higher standard than it has reached elsewhere. Green engineered for the desert, a podium that is genuinely public, a programme sized to the market.

New

The combination, at this scale and in this climate, has not been built. The newness is in the synthesis of proven parts.
